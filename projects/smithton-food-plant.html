Smithton, Australia · Food Processing Plant

$38,000,000 FM, UL, and FDA qualified food processing facility with temperature controlled potato storage and two new production lines.

Owner’s Representative with design build oversight. Delivered with a 0.00 Recordable Incident Rate.

Project Overview

CSC served as Owner’s Representative with design build oversight for a McCain Foods processing program in Smithton, Tasmania. Scope centered on a temperature controlled potato storage warehouse and two new production and packaging lines. CSC led design coordination, value engineering, budget development, bid documents and contractor evaluations, safety adherence, and day to day construction management.

Program Value & Scale

  • Program value: approx. $38,000,000.
  • Facility: temperature controlled potato storage.
  • Schedule: phased commissioning while maintaining operations.

Key Deliverables

  • Temperature controlled potato warehouse (extended storage time strategy).
  • Two new food production and packaging lines.
  • Civil upgrades including employee parking and utility work.

Execution

  • Design coordination and value engineering.
  • Budget forecasting and bid package authoring.
  • Safety management; special inspections (steel, concrete, tilt up panels).
  • Schedule, quality, and cost control.

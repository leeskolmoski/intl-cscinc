Smithton, Australia · Food Processing Plant

$38,000,000 FM (Factory Mutual), UL (Underwriters Laboratories), and FDA (Food and Drug Administration) qualified food processing facility with temperature controlled potato storage and two new production lines.

Owner’s Representative with design build oversight; project delivered with a 0.00 Recordable Incident Rate.

Project Overview

CSC served as Owner’s Representative with design build oversight for a McCain Foods processing program in Smithton, Tasmania. Scope centered on a temperature controlled potato storage warehouse and two new production and packaging lines, with mechanical design focused on extending storage times while minimizing weight loss. CSC led design coordination, value engineering, budget development and forecasting, bid documents and contractor evaluations, safety adherence, special inspections for steel and concrete including tilt up panels, and day to day construction management. Responsibilities also included schedule control, QA/QC, and civil upgrades for new employee parking and utilities. The project achieved a 0.00 Recordable Incident Rate.

Program Value and Scale

  • Program value: approx. $38,000,000.
  • Facility: food processing with temperature controlled potato storage.
  • Schedule: coordinated phasing to maintain operations and commissioning.

Key Deliverables

  • Two new food production and packaging lines.
  • Civil and site upgrades, including employee parking and utility services.

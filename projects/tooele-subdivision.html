Tooele, Utah · Subdivision

$66,000,000, 220-lot civil subdivision with five community shared buildings.

CSC managed full civil delivery for this phased subdivision, including grading, utilities, roads, and stormwater systems, with shared community facilities and amenities.

Project Overview

The Tooele subdivision encompasses 220 residential lots and five community shared buildings. CSC oversaw all civil work including grading, underground utilities, stormwater drainage, asphalt, curb, gutter, and sidewalks. The phased approach accelerated lot releases while maintaining QA/QC standards for long-term asset performance.

Park City, Utah · Mixed-Use Development

$44,000,000 design/build mixed-use project featuring five above-grade stories and three levels of subgrade parking.

CSC served as General Contractor for this complex urban build in the heart of Park City, coordinating design/build delivery, tenant integration, and municipal permitting within a tight mountain footprint.

Project Overview

This $44,000,000 mixed-use facility combines residential, retail, and parking into a single five-story structure with three subgrade levels. CSC managed all construction under a design/build delivery model, ensuring structural precision, adherence to mountain environmental constraints, and efficient scheduling throughout the build.

Irapuato, Mexico · Distribution & Storage

$32,900,000 · 274,000 ft² distribution and storage facility.

Delivered turnkey from concept through commissioning for ConAgra Foods, including developer prequalification, design, construction, and full safety and export-compliance management.

Project Overview

CSC managed a greenfield distribution and storage facility in Irapuato, Mexico. The site features ambient and cold storage, cross-dock functionality, and a large truck-yard expansion. CSC led developer prequalification and lease negotiations with nine firms before directing design, permitting, utilities, and onsite construction. The team authored ConAgra Foods’ facility specification standard, developed safety policies, and coordinated commissioning and training while maintaining strict export compliance throughout.

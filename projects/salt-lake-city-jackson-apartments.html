Salt Lake City, Utah · Jackson Apartments

$34,000,000 renovation of a 70-unit residential complex, including seismic and structural modernization.

CSC served as General Contractor for the historic Jackson Apartments, built in 1920. The program joined two buildings into one cohesive 70-unit residential structure through seismic retrofitting, modernization, and historic preservation.

Project Overview

The Jackson Apartments renovation delivered modern living standards while retaining the architectural identity of a century-old structure. CSC managed extensive seismic strengthening, structural tie-ins, and modernization of building systems across both towers. All work was performed under active occupancy constraints and historic review oversight.

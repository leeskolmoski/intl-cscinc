$9,200,000 program. Full civil and utilities with municipal tie-ins, curb, gutter, sidewalk, and asphalt roads.

A 16-lot subdivision in Midway, Utah, delivered through design/build. Scope included grading, underground utilities, stormwater conveyance and retention, roadway section, curb and gutter, sidewalks, and municipal tie-ins. CSC managed schedule, budget, and QA/QC through turnover.

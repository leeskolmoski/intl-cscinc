CSC steps in as the owner’s representative to run the project. We coordinate design, manage budget and schedule, and keep the work aligned with food grade, cold storage, or industrial requirements.

Services built for complex work

Every project needs clear responsibility. CSC provides that. We front load planning, keep contractors coordinated, and document the project so turnover is clean.

The services below are the ones we deliver most often. We can scale them up for multi-site programs or keep them focused for a single facility expansion.

Owner’s Representative

Single point of contact for the owner. We manage design, schedule, budget, and risk and report progress in a way executives can use.

  • Program definition and budgeting
  • Design coordination and constructability review
  • Permitting and municipal interface
  • Vendor and contractor management
  • Change order evaluation

Construction Management

Field oversight, safety focus, submittal tracking, and coordination so work can continue without bottlenecks.

  • Preconstruction planning
  • Subcontractor coordination
  • Safety leadership and site monitoring
  • Schedule management and reporting
  • Cost tracking and forecasting

Design-Build Oversight

Integrates designer and builder, keeps value engineering aligned with operations, and protects start-up dates.

  • RFP creation and bid evaluation
  • Design management and VE tracking
  • Schedule and cost control
  • QA/QC, commissioning, and turnover

Food and Beverage Facilities

Process piping, clean room, cold storage and USDA requirements.

  • Sanitary/hygienic design coordination
  • CIP/SIP systems interface
  • Environmental and USDA compliance
  • Process equipment integration
  • Sequenced construction for active operations

Cold Storage and Logistics

Distribution infrastructure, dock coordination and temperature-controlled operations.

  • Site logistics and circulation
  • Refrigeration/insulated envelope integration
  • Dock equipment + MHE coordination
  • Fire/life safety for high-pile storage
  • Phased tie-ins to existing distribution

Document Control and Closeout

RFIs, submittals, safety reports and final as-built packages.

  • Digital RFI and submittal tracking
  • Daily reporting and photo logs
  • Safety and inspection records
  • Owner training documentation
  • Turnover/closeout manuals

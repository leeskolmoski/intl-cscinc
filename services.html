Construction Services Consulting, Inc.

Our Services

Owner’s Representative, Design-Build Oversight, and Construction Management, set up for food and beverage, cold storage, industrial, and infrastructure work.

Owner’s Representative

CSC acts as an extension of ownership. We manage design, budget, schedule, and risk from the moment a project is defined, so the owner can stay focused on operations.

  • Program definition and budgeting
  • Team assembly and contracting
  • Design coordination and constructability
  • Permitting and municipal interface
  • Procurement and vendor management
  • Detailed field planning to maintain quality and flow
  • Change order evaluation

Design-Build Oversight

We coordinate all design and construction phases to keep speed, accuracy, quality, and accountability aligned. This is especially important when production or cold storage start-up dates cannot move.

  • RFP creation and bid evaluation
  • Design management and value engineering
  • Schedule and cost control
  • QA/QC, commissioning, and turnover

Construction Management

CSC delivers experienced CM support for large scale, multi-discipline programs. We keep subcontractors coordinated, we keep the owner informed, and we keep the work progressing.

  • Preconstruction planning
  • Budget and cost forecasting
  • Subcontractor coordination
  • Safety leadership and site monitoring
  • Schedule management and progress reporting

Specialized Expertise

Much of our work is in food and beverage, cold storage, manufacturing, and sites that have to meet USDA, FDA, or SQF requirements. We help owners keep production-first priorities inside construction-first timelines.

  • Food and beverage facilities (USDA, FDA, SQF)
  • Cold storage and logistics
  • Manufacturing and clean rooms
  • Office and mixed-use development
  • Wastewater treatment and utilities
  • Subdivision and infrastructure

Document Control and Site Monitoring

Owners and lenders want clean records. We build that into the service, not as an afterthought.

  • Document control and construction reporting
  • Field photo and progress documentation
  • Site supervision and monitoring
  • Closeout packages for future maintenance
